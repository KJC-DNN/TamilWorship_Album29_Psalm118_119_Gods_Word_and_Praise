# Psalm 119:18 – “Open my eyes, that I may see wondrous things in Your law.” A direct prayer for spiritual illumination. The psalmist, though surrounded by God’s Word, asks for divine help to truly perceive its depth, beauty, and treasures. Key theme: Without God’s light, the Bible remains closed to our hearts.

Closing Prayer:
Lord Jesus, open my eyes that I may see the wondrous things in Your law. Remove every veil of spiritual blindness, hardness of heart, and worldly distractions. Give me fresh revelation and understanding as I read Your Word. Amen.

Worship Focus:
Seeking God’s revelation and enlightenment
Developing deep hunger and thirst for the Scriptures
Finding joy and delight in God’s testimonies and commands
Moving from head knowledge to heart transformation
